A make.fun entry for "The 3 AM Excuse" — one line, under 200 characters, in which a hardware wallet refuses to sign a transaction at 3 AM.

## The entry

> It's 3 AM — I'm not signing, I'm clocking off for beauty sleep, and so are you. Your coins will still be here at sunrise; your judgment won't.

142 characters, 1 line. The wallet is tired too and refuses on wellness grounds — it says the refusal outright, clocks off for beauty sleep and sends its human to bed with it, because the coins will survive until sunrise but the human's 3 AM judgment will not.

python3 main.py
